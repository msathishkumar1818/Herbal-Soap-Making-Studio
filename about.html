The Studio Creed

Our Four Uncompromising Botanical Standards

Every batch crafted in our atelier follows a rigorous ethical protocol that honors the flora, the soil, and your dermal wellness.

01 • Purity

Zero Detergents

Free from SLS, palm oil, parabens, synthetic hardeners, and phthalates. 100% genuine cold-saponified plant lather.

Certified Dermatologically Pure
02 • Foraging

Wild Harvested

Herbs are collected by hand at peak morning bloom, ensuring perennial root systems remain thriving for future seasons.

Regenerative Wildcraft Protocol
03 • Patience

42-Day Cedar Aging

We never rush curing with electric ovens. Every bar gently dehydrates on native cedar racks until it reaches mild equilibrium.

Retains 100% Natural Glycerin
04 • Earth First

Plantable Seed Paper

Zero plastic films. All packaging is printed on post-consumer recycled paper embedded with wildflower seeds you can plant.

100% Circular & Biodegradable

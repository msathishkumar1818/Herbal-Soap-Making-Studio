Micro-Seasonal Harvests

The Soapmaker’s Botanical Almanac

We align our soap batches with the natural blossoming cycles of mountain flora to extract volatile plant essences at their therapeutic zenith.

Spring • Months 03–05

Blossom Solar Maceration

Chamomile, elderflower, and wild dandelions steeped 30 days in pure cold-pressed sweet almond oil.

Summer • Months 06–08

Lavender Steam Distillation

Fresh purple lavender sprigs harvested at daybreak and steam-extracted into pure floral water.

Autumn • Months 09–11

Mountain Clay Mining

French green montmorillonite and rose kaolin sun-sifted for silk-smooth lather texture.

Winter • Months 12–02

Cedarwood Pressing

Aged cedar resin and fir needle distillation creating deep grounding therapeutic notes.

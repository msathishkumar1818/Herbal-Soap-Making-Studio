The Slow Method

Why Real Cold-Process Soap Demands 42 Days

Commercial soaps use chemical detergents and flash-dry within minutes. At our studio, we practice traditional saponification that retains all natural plant glycerin.

01

Botanical Infusion & Lye Blend

Raw unrefined oils and concentrated herb waters are mixed at room temperature to preserve fragile phytonutrients.

02

Hand-Pouring & Mold Insulating

The soap emulsion is hand-poured into lined wood molds, kept warm under cotton blankets for 48 hours.

03

Piano Wire Slicing & Hand Stamping

Blocks are trimmed and sliced using tensioned wire, individually stamped with our studio seal.

04

6-Week Cedarwood Rack Curing

Bars rest in our humidity-controlled curing room for 6 weeks, developing a long-lasting, ultra-mild density.

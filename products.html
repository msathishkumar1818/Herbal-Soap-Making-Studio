Care & Storage

Make Natural Soap Last 50% Longer

Zero chemical hardeners. 4 simple daily habits to double lifespan.

01

Slotted Cedar Dish

Allows 360° airflow to prevent standing puddle mush.

02

Sisal Agave Pouch

Hangs in shower to drip-dry fast with gentle exfoliation.

03

Rotate Dual Bars

24-hour drying between uses hardens the bar density.

04

Cut Bar in Halves

Store second half dry in linen drawer while using first.
